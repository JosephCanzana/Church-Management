{% comment %} dashboards/_chart_columns.html Grouped columns (men and women by age group), drawn as inline SVG from charts.column_groups(). Pass: span (grid-span classes from the bento grid, optional), chart (prepared dict: groups, y_ticks, baseline_y, plot, width, height, empty, series, title, caption, aria) and empty_text. Each group is one age group; each bar in it is one series (men, women), colored with the chart_slot colors and always named in the legend under the chart. Every bar prints its value above it and has a tooltip with group, series and value.
