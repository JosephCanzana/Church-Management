{% comment %} tithes/print.html Standalone A4 "Reports on Tithes & Offering" for the browser's print dialog ("Save as PDF" is a print destination). It does NOT extend base_app.html: black text on white whatever the person's light or dark mode, inline CSS, no external fonts, no Tailwind, no includes/logo.html. The layout copies the paper form: church and extension, date, No. of Tithers, Tithes, Offering, Total, a numbered Name and Amount grid, the bills-and-coins breakdown (1000 to 1), TOTAL AMOUNT, the difference line, Prepared by and two Verified by signature lines. Context (tithes.views.report_print): report, sheet, is_draft, totals, difference_abs, rows, denominations, bypass_note, back_url. Never a birth date, phone number or address, and never a decision note. Printing notes: thead repeats on every page and rows never split (no page numbers: CSS page counters are unreliable across browsers).
