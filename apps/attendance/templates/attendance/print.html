{% comment %} attendance/print.html Standalone A4 attendance sheet for the browser's print dialog ("Save as PDF" is a print destination). It does NOT extend base_app.html: black text on white whatever the person's light or dark mode, no external fonts, no Tailwind. The layout copies the paper sheet: one numbered list, a tick in the gender and age-network column, a G (guest) and an FT column, then the summary and the age-network legend. The sheet fills the whole A4 page: the summary and signatures sit at the bottom of the page, and the rows are a little taller than before. Context (attendance.views.sheet_print): sheet, summary, table, rows, heads, back_url. Privacy: names, gender and age network only. Never a birth date, phone number or address. Printing notes: thead repeats on every page and rows never split (no page numbers: CSS page counters are unreliable across browsers).
