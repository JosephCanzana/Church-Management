Columns

Required: first_name, last_name, gender, extension. Gender is male or female. The extension is matched by name, ignoring capitals and extra spaces; it must already exist and not be archived.

Optional: middle_name, suffix, birth_date, person_type, phone_number and the address columns. Write dates as YYYY-MM-DD. person_type is member (default, can sign in), member_record (no account) or guest. Bulk add creates members only; add coordinators and admins from the People screen.

Members who can sign in get the default password that applies to you (or a generated one) and must change it at first sign-in. The passwords are shown once after the import.
