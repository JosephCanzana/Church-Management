Welcome to the JIL Church Management System. This System is designed to help Jesus Is Lord (JIL) Church manage membership records, attendance, church events, record Tithes Offering and other administrative activities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, who may access it, and what rights you have over your personal information. We handle personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and other applicable laws of the Philippines.

1. What Information We Collect

We collect information necessary for church administration and the proper operation of the System.

Account Information. When an account is created, we may collect your name, username, email address, contact number, and other details required for account management.

Church Membership Information. We may collect membership details, attendance records, and participation in church services and activities.

System Activity Information. We may record login activity, account roles, and relevant system logs to help maintain security and prevent unauthorized access.

We collect only information that is necessary for the purposes disclosed to you and permitted by law.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Manage church membership records and member accounts.
  • Monitor attendance at worship services and church activities.
  • Organize ministries, events, and church programs.
  • Send relevant announcements and updates.
  • Generate reports for authorized church administrators.
  • Maintain system security and prevent unauthorized access.

We do not use members' information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

3. How We Share Your Information

Your information may be accessed by authorized church administrators and personnel who need it to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Information may also be shared with service providers that support the System, or with appropriate authorities when required or permitted by law.

We do not make private membership records publicly available or sell personal information for commercial purposes.

4. Where We Store Your Information

Your information is stored using the database and hosting services configured for the JIL Church Management System.

Access to stored information should be restricted to authorized personnel. Appropriate security measures must be implemented to protect records against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

The church should identify its actual hosting provider and storage location and provide additional information about data transfers when applicable.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Under applicable data privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission when appropriate.

You may contact the church administrator or designated privacy contact to exercise these rights, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

6. How We Protect Your Information

We use or seek to implement appropriate security measures, such as account authentication, access restrictions, secure data handling, backups, and system monitoring.

Users must keep their login credentials confidential and must not access or share records without authorization.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for church administration, recordkeeping, and applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed and no lawful retention requirement applies, it should be securely deleted, anonymized, or disposed of.

8. Privacy Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when the System's features, data processing practices, or legal requirements change.

Significant changes will be communicated through appropriate church channels or system notifications when appropriate.
